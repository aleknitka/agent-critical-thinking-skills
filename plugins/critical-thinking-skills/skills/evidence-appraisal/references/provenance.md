# Provenance — Evidence Appraisal

## Traces

- Facione (1990) Delphi: evaluation and inference as core CT skills; source credibility as evaluation sub-skill.
- Paul & Elder (2006/2019) elements of thought: *information* — accuracy, relevance, sufficiency of evidence.
- Ioannidis (2005) "Why most published research findings are false" — base for tier skepticism and bias discounting.
- Halpern (2014) Ch. 4 — evaluating evidence and claims.
- OpenAlex `W4407*` methods vocabulary — tier hints for helpers (see `scripts/evidence.py`).

## What this skill is not

- Not a literature-search service. It scores the source you bring; it does not find one.
- Not a statistical re-analysis. Tier and effect are taken as stated; misreporting is not caught.
- Strength 1-5 is a heuristic, not a measurement. It is comparable *within* a decision, not across fields.

## Honesty note

No RCT isolates "evidence-appraisal training via this scorecard" as a proven creativity or decision boost. Like the 11 research extensions in the creative track, this is a structured heuristic presented with the same disclosure. Sio & Lortie-Forgues (2024) meta: raw g~0.53 deflates to ~0.30 after bias correction — keep expectations modest and practice-bound.
